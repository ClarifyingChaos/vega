Vega: test build

Thanks for trying Vega. It's an early build, shared for day-to-day testing and feedback.

NEEDS
  A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later), macOS 15.5 or later.

INSTALL
  1. Drag Vega into the Applications folder in this window.
  2. Eject the Vega disk.

FIRST OPEN (once)
  Vega isn't from the App Store and isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS stops it the first time.
  1. Open Vega from Applications. macOS says it can't verify the developer. Click Done.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to Vega.
  3. Confirm with your password or Touch ID. After this it opens normally.

  If macOS instead says Vega "is damaged", run this once in Terminal, then open it again:
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Vega.app

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Vega updates itself: it checks for a new version in the background and installs it on
    the next relaunch. Vega menu > Check for Updates… checks now.
  - Passwords: Vega saves and fills logins itself (Settings > Passwords, locked with Touch ID).
  - Sync: Settings > General > Sync keeps your spaces, favorites and passwords the same on your Macs,
    through iCloud Drive or a Google account. Not in this build: web push notifications.
  - Import from another browser: Vega menu > Import from Another Browser.
  - Everything stays on your Mac. Nothing is sent to us.

FEEDBACK
  Anything that breaks, feels slow, or that you wish it did. A screenshot helps.
  Issues: https://github.com/ClarifyingChaos/vega/issues
